# ChessGPT ♟️

**ChessGPT** is an experimental AI research project exploring whether a neural network can learn to understand chess positions, select legal moves, and develop chess-playing abilities without relying on a conventional chess engine for its core decision-making.

The project focuses on learning chess through data, neural networks, and experimentation rather than hard-coded chess strategies or traditional engine search.

## 🎯 Objectives

- **Position Understanding:** Explore ways to represent chess positions as model inputs.
- **Legal Move Prediction:** Train a model to produce legal moves from a given position.
- **Chess Learning:** Investigate how neural networks can learn patterns, positional concepts, and decision-making through training.
- **Explainable Reasoning:** Explore ways for the model to communicate the reasoning behind its moves.
- **Playing Strength:** Gradually improve the model's ability to play competitive chess.

## 🧠 Approach

ChessGPT aims to investigate a neural-network-driven approach to chess, starting with a relatively small model and expanding as experiments provide evidence for doing so.

Board representation, training data, model architecture, and evaluation methods are all part of the ongoing research. The initial focus is on understanding the fundamentals and establishing a reliable baseline before pursuing more ambitious goals.

## 🚧 Project Status

ChessGPT is in its early research and development stage. Its architecture and methods are evolving, and the objectives above represent research goals rather than completed capabilities.

**Active development takes place in the [`develop`](https://github.com/Roboticela/ChessGPT/tree/develop)[ branch](https://github.com/Roboticela/ChessGPT/tree/develop).** Visit it to explore ongoing work, experiments, and the latest development progress. Content in this branch may be incomplete or subject to change.

## 🔬 Research Philosophy

The project prioritizes experimentation, measurable progress, and learning from failure. Ideas will be tested rather than assumed to work, and conclusions will be guided by results.

## 📌 Long-Term Vision

Develop a chess-playing model that learns to interpret positions and make decisions through its own learned representations, with the long-term ambition of achieving strong play and meaningful explanations of its decisions.
